# Contributing

This is an Iowa-specific open educational resource, not a legal advice service. Contributions are welcome for factual corrections, accessibility, instructional design, and fictional exercises.

1. Search existing issues; identify the file, section and revision.
2. For procedural claims, cite the controlling Iowa statute, rule, official form or court guidance, including section, effective date and access date. Distinguish binding authority from guidance.
3. Explain the proposed correction and its educational impact. Preserve neutral treatment of plaintiffs and defendants.
4. Submit a focused pull request to develop/curriculum. Include source evidence and any affected exercises, answer keys or cross-references.
5. Maintainer review is required; legal review is separate. Do not mark material attorney-reviewed without documented qualified review.

Do not post real case records, personal data, requests for individual legal advice or confidential information. Use invented examples. By submitting original educational content for inclusion, you agree to license it under CC BY-SA 4.0 and confirm you have the necessary rights.

See [review policy](REVIEW.md) and [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
