# Editorial and legal review policy

**Status: public alpha; professional Iowa legal review outstanding.** Publishing on main is not a formal release or certification.

## Review workflow

For each substantive procedural statement, record the module/section, proposition, primary authority and pinpoint, effective date, date checked, reviewer role, status and any unresolved discrepancy. Use the [review register](review-register.md). Mark unchecked or disputed claims accordingly; never infer legal approval from automated checks.

Check Iowa Code chapter 631, applicable court rules, current official forms and court guidance. Verify deadlines, service, jurisdiction, fees, default, appeals, judgment enforcement and satisfaction particularly carefully. Where sources conflict, identify the issue and seek qualified Iowa counsel rather than guessing.

Editorial review includes accessibility, neutral treatment of both parties, fictional labeling, arithmetic, cross-links and consistency between student and instructor materials. Recheck affected content after legal changes and before any release. A qualified Iowa attorney's documented review and owner approval remain release gates; record scope, date and limitations of any such review. Do not imply endorsement.

Corrections affecting learner safety should be prominently flagged pending resolution. Review dates are not guarantees of current law.
