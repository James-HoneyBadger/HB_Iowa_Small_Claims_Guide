# Code of conduct

Treat participants respectfully regardless of identity, background, legal experience or role in a dispute. Critique claims and sources, not people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, solicitation of confidential case details and personal attacks are unacceptable.

Keep discussions educational and neutral; do not provide individualized legal advice or pressure anyone to disclose their case. Report concerns privately using the maintainer's published GitHub profile contact options; do not post sensitive details publicly. Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation or close discussions. Decisions should be proportionate and protect privacy.
