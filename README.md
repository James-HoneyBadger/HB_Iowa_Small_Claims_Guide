# Honey Badger Guide to Understanding the Iowa Small Claims Court

**IMPORTANT — EDUCATIONAL MATERIAL ONLY. NOTHING IN THIS PROJECT IS LEGAL ADVICE.** Seek qualified, licensed Iowa legal counsel for any clarification or questions about your own circumstances. Verify current statutes, court rules, forms and orders before acting. This is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by any court, government agency or organization.

## Purpose

Legal literacy. Procedural understanding. Informed citizenship.

An Iowa-specific, documentation-first curriculum for adults learning about ordinary small claims money actions, including self-representation, evidence, filing, hearings, judgments and closure. Small claims courts generally aim to offer a simpler forum for modest civil disputes, but rules vary by jurisdiction; this project does not teach other states' procedures.

## Development status

Public working project. The curriculum is public alpha material on `main`, with ongoing development on `develop/curriculum`. Public availability is not a beta designation or formal release. No formal release or claim of legal authority. Professional legal review and owner approval remain outstanding.

All classroom cases are fictional. Do not submit simulated documents to a court. Instructors provide general education only, not individualized advice or representation.
